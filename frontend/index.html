Quick checks before you click

  • Check the senderCompare the full email address, not just the display name. Be cautious of misspellings and unexpected requests.
  • Inspect links safelyHover to preview a link on desktop, or press and hold to preview on mobile. Check the actual domain and do not open links you do not trust.
  • Pause on urgencyUnexpected password, payment, gift-card, or verification requests should be confirmed through a known official channel—not by replying to the message.
